# Dataset register

This register is a research starting point, not an endorsement or guarantee of access. Re-check current terms, provenance and third-party rights before downloading or using any dataset.

| Candidate | Reported content | Possible use | Caveats |
|---|---|---|---|
| [EdgeVision Dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/j82bnw7gsr/1) | Publisher describes 2,392 images and 8,275 boxes across BikeWithRider, NoHelmet and Helmet | Initial image pipeline validation | Not established as representative highway CCTV; inspect archive and licence |
| [AI City Challenge 2024 Track 5](https://www.aicitychallenge.org/2024-data-and-evaluation/) | Video and rider/helmet annotations | Video-level benchmark if access is granted | Access and dataset agreement may be required |
| [Indian Traffic Helmet Violation](https://huggingface.co/datasets/thundarstrom/traffic-helmet-violation) | Dataset card claims two classes and CC BY 4.0 metadata | Candidate only after verification | Verify actual files, provenance and terms independently |
| [Motorcycle Driver Helmet Use](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/bmy35m25pw/1) | Controlled motorcycle-driver videos | Supplementary visual research | Controlled scenes are not representative highway surveillance |

Do not commit third-party datasets or identifiable footage to this repository. A dataset metadata licence does not override privacy, publicity or other third-party rights.
